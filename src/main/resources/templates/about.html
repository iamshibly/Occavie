OUR STORY

Great experiences start with showing up.

Finding the right event should not mean scrolling through chats and posters. And running one should not mean juggling spreadsheets, payment screenshots and paper lists.

Occavie puts it all in one place: a clear way to discover events, book in a few taps, and walk in with a ticket on your phone, plus a complete toolkit for the people hosting them.

BUILT FOR EVERYONE INVOLVED

One platform, three workspaces

Participants

Discover and attend with confidence.

  • Search in plain sentences and compare events
  • Book free or paid tickets, join waitlists
  • Instant PDF tickets with QR codes
  • Saved events, calendar links, reminders

Organizers

Plan, sell and run events end to end.

  • Guided event wizard with drafts and preview
  • Ticket types, coupons and capacity control
  • QR check-in at the door
  • Analytics, CSV exports and a Copilot readiness score

Admins

Keep the whole platform healthy.

  • Manage users, roles and events
  • Approve events before they go live
  • Moderate reviews and answer support
  • Platform-wide analytics

Private
