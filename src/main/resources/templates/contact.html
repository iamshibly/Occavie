How do I get my ticket?

After booking, open My Registrations in your dashboard and download the PDF ticket. It includes a QR code to scan at the door.

I paid but my payment was not accepted

Payments on Occavie are a demonstration. Use the reference shown at checkout exactly as displayed. If it still fails, send us the event name and what you saw.

How do I host my own event?

Create a free account, choose Host events, and the event wizard walks you through details, tickets and publishing. The Copilot shows what to improve.

Can I get a refund or cancel a booking?

Free bookings can be cancelled from your registrations. For anything else, send a request with the event name and we will point you to the organizer.

I forgot my password

Use Forgot password on the login page. We email you a secure link that works once for 30 minutes.
